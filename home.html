About us

A few kitchens.
A lot of stories.

From line cooking in Holland to setting up kitchens in Spain. From South African braais to Michelin-starred kitchens.

We’ve learned from different kitchens, cultures and people along the way. Now, it’s time to bring all of that experience together and share it with the world — in our own way.

Fork It is about good food, relaxed tables and bringing people together. No matter who you are, where you’re from or whether you know everyone at the table when you arrive — there’s a seat for you.

Because we believe the best meals are rarely just about the food. They’re about the people you meet, the conversations you have and the memories you make.

So, pull up a chair. We’d love to meet you.

Fun fact: “paella” is Valencian for “pan” — the dish is named after the pan it’s cooked in.

Paella was born as a farmers’ dish in Valencia, Spain.

The crispy, caramelised rice at the bottom is called the socarrat — and it’s the best part.
