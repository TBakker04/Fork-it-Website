FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you might want to know before booking — if your question isn't here, just email or message us.

How far in advance do I need to book?

Book at least 1 week in advance. For larger or more involved events — like a Private Dinner with a fully personalised menu — we recommend reaching out a bit earlier so there’s enough time for planning and sourcing ingredients.

How far from Leiden do you travel?

We cook anywhere in South Holland (Zuid-Holland) — Leiden and the surrounding region, as well as further out toward The Hague, Rotterdam or Amsterdam. Not sure if your location is covered? Just ask.

What if someone in my group has an allergy or dietary requirement?

Our Date Dinner’s seasonal menu is fixed, but we can make exceptions if one or two guests in your group have an allergy. For a Private Dinner, we build the menu fully around you, so we can always accommodate allergies and dietary wishes there. Either way, let us know when you book so we can see what’s possible.

How does payment work?

You pay the full amount upfront, when you confirm your booking.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Cancel up to 3 days before your event and you’ll get a full refund. After that, refunds unfortunately aren’t possible anymore — get in touch and we’ll look at the options together.

Do I need to arrange anything myself, like a kitchen?

We bring most things ourselves — tableware, ingredients and cooking equipment. For Private Dinners we cook in your own space, so you’ll need a kitchen and stove we can use. Not sure if your space works? Let us know and we’ll figure it out together.

How many guests can you cater for?

It varies by event — our Paella Workshops, for example, start from 5 guests, while a Date Dinner or Private Dinner can work for a smaller group too. Not sure if your headcount fits? Send us a message and we’ll see what’s possible.

Still have a question?

Email or message us — we usually reply the same day.
